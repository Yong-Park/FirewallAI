# ADR 0003: AI advisor ships in v0.2, not v0.1

- **Status:** Accepted. Explicit human roadmap (rev. 2 feedback, 2026-10-14): "V0.1: Read-only Firewall Auditor with early GUI, deterministic findings, scan history, and basic reports. V0.2: Local Ollama AI Advisor…".
- **Decision ID:** D3 · **Date:** 2026-10-14 · **Proposed by:** claude-code · **Decided by:** human, 2026-10-14

## 1. Problem
If the AI advisor and the audit engine land in the same release, the AI has nothing well tested to explain. Bugs would also be harder to pin down, since a wrong answer could come from the collectors or from the model.

## 2. Decision (recommended)
v0.1 is deterministic and makes **zero outbound network calls**. The AI advisor (explain-only, Ollama) ships in v0.2, built on v0.1's validated findings.

## 3. Alternatives considered
- **AI in v0.1.** Faster to demo, but the AI would explain unvalidated data. The DoD would also lose its "zero network" guarantee, and the scope would grow.
- **AI in v0.3, together with RAG.** Delays the core differentiator too long.

## 4. Advantages and disadvantages
- **Advantages:** a smaller, safer v0.1 whose behavior can be tested. The AI receives a stable evidence model.
- **Disadvantages:** the first release has no AI features.

## 5. Security implications
- v0.1's attack surface excludes prompt injection and any risk of data leaving the machine.
- v0.2 adds a threat-model update before release.

## 6. Performance implications
None for v0.1.

## 7. Validation
The v0.1 DoD includes a zero-egress test: the test suite fails on any socket use, and a manual Resource Monitor check is done before release.

## 8. What to learn
Incremental delivery, and why deterministic baselines come before probabilistic components.
